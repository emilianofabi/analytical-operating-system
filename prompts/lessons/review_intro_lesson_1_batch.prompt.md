# Review Introductory Lesson 1 Batch

Use this prompt to review a small batch of AOS introductory Lesson 1 artifacts.

## Review Scope

Review only the intended introductory Lesson 1 files in the batch.

Do not modify files during review unless explicitly asked to revise after the review.

## Checks

For each lesson, check whether:

- the lesson is introductory, not overbuilt
- the central question is clear
- concepts are appropriate for Lesson 1
- the mini-artifact is small and visible
- the career translation is safe
- the overclaiming warning is present
- "what this lesson does not prove yet" is present
- closure criteria are realistic
- the lesson does not claim mastery
- the lesson does not claim review, audit, or closure prematurely
- the lesson can be reviewed, revised, and audited later
- no unrelated files were modified
- no closed artifacts were modified
- no learner-state files were modified
- no frontend files were modified
- no RAG files were modified

## Output Format

```text
PASS / NEEDS_REVISION / FAIL

Defects:
Required fixes:
Overclaiming risks:
Scope risks:
Closure readiness:
Recommended next action:
```
