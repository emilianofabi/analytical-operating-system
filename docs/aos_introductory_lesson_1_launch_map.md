# AOS Introductory Lesson 1 Launch Map

## Purpose

This map defines the scaffold for introductory Lesson 1 artifacts across the major AOS learning modules. It is a planning and execution guide for creating small, coherent doors into the curriculum, not a request to generate full lessons for every module at once.

The goal is to support future closed lesson loops:

artifact -> review -> revision -> audit -> closure

## Definition of an Introductory Lesson 1

An introductory Lesson 1 orients the learner to a module, names one central question, introduces a few starting concepts, and asks for one small visible output.

Lesson 1 is not mastery; Lesson 1 is orientation plus one visible output.

An introductory Lesson 1 should:

- stay small enough to review
- avoid polished mastery claims
- produce one mini-artifact
- include one safe career translation
- include one overclaiming warning
- remain open until review, revision, audit, and closure are complete

## Lesson Output Template

Each introductory Lesson 1 should use:

```text
templates/lessons/intro_lesson_1_template.md
```

Each lesson should include:

- module name
- lesson status
- central question
- plain-language explanation
- key concepts
- first mental model
- system analogy
- mini-artifact
- career translation
- overclaiming warning
- what the lesson does not prove yet
- review questions
- closure criteria
- safe career claim
- notes or open questions

## Batch Rule

Do not generate all 18 full lessons at once.

Generate small batches only, ideally no more than four modules at a time. A batch should be small enough for review, revision, audit, and closure to happen without creating many open lesson loops.

## Closure Rule

No introductory Lesson 1 should be described as reviewed, audited, closed, portfolio-ready, or proof of mastery until it has actually passed through:

1. artifact creation
2. review
3. revision
4. audit
5. closure

Draft lesson files are orientation artifacts. They are not final evidence.
