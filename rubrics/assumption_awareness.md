# Rubric: Assumption-Awareness in Technical Work

## Purpose

This rubric evaluates whether a student identifies, justifies, tests, and communicates the assumptions that make technical work meaningful.

## Scale

1. Emerging
2. Developing
3. Proficient
4. Advanced

## Criteria

| Criterion | 1 Emerging | 2 Developing | 3 Proficient | 4 Advanced |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Problem Framing | Problem is vague or tool-driven. | Problem is bounded but incompletely specified. | Problem, purpose, audience, and claim are clear. | Problem framing anticipates alternative formulations and stakes. |
| Assumption Identification | Assumptions are mostly implicit. | Assumptions are listed but not analyzed. | Assumptions are connected to method and interpretation. | Assumptions are prioritized by fragility, consequence, and testability. |
| Data-Generating Process | Treats data as given. | Mentions data source but not production process. | Explains provenance, measurement, missingness, and selection. | Analyzes how institutional processes shape what the data can mean. |
| Method Fit | Method is selected by familiarity or convenience. | Method partially fits the problem. | Method fits the question and assumptions are stated. | Method is defended against plausible alternatives. |
| Diagnostics and Sensitivity | Minimal checking. | Standard checks applied mechanically. | Diagnostics correspond to key assumptions. | Diagnostics and sensitivity analyses target the most consequential assumptions. |
| Limitation Articulation | Limitations are generic or absent. | Some relevant limitations are stated. | Limitations are tied to conclusions. | Limitations define scope, credibility, and future evidence needs. |

## Evidence Requirements

Assessors should look for:

- problem statement;
- method section;
- data documentation;
- diagnostics;
- limitation section;
- code or technical appendix;
- oral defense responses.

## Calibration Protocol

Reviewers should score two anchor artifacts before formal scoring. Disagreements greater than one level on any criterion require discussion and written justification.
