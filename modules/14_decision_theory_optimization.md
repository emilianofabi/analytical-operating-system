# Module 14: Decision Theory, Optimization, Operations Research, and Strategic Decision-Making

## Track
Methods & Systems

## Credit Hours
4

## Core Purpose

Cultivate decision judgment: the ability to choose actions under uncertainty, constraints, competing objectives, incomplete information, risk, and institutional consequences.

## Governing Principles

1. A prediction is not a decision.
2. Every decision requires alternatives.
3. Every optimization requires an objective function.
4. Objectives encode values.
5. Constraints define the feasible set.
6. Expected value must be interpreted with risk.
7. More information is valuable only if it can change the decision.
8. Robustness may matter more than theoretical optimality.

## Core Questions

- What decision is actually being made?
- What alternatives are available?
- What objective is being optimized?
- What constraints bind the decision?
- What uncertainty matters?
- What information would change the decision?
- What risks are hidden in averages?
- What tradeoffs cannot be avoided?
- What decision rule is justified?
