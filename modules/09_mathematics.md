# Module 09: Mathematics for Economics, Statistics, Machine Learning, and Systems Thinking

## Track
Foundational

## Credit Hours

## Core Purpose

Cultivate mathematical structure: the ability to use algebra, calculus, linear algebra, probability, optimization, graphs, and numerical methods as languages for modeling relationships, change, uncertainty, and computation.

## Governing Principles

1. Mathematics is a language of structure.
2. Formal notation should clarify meaning, not obscure it.
3. Every equation is a claim about relationships.
4. Derivatives express marginal change.
5. Linear algebra represents systems compactly.
6. Probability formalizes uncertainty.
7. Optimization formalizes tradeoffs.
8. Computation makes formal reasoning executable.

## Core Questions

- What relationship is being represented?
- What variables matter?
- What changes when a variable changes?
- What structure can be represented as a vector, matrix, graph, or function?
- What is being optimized?
- What constraints bind the problem?
- What uncertainty is formalized?
- What approximation is being made?
