# Module 23: Public Policy, Regulation, Governance, and Institutional Design

## Track
Integration & Judgment

## Credit Hours
4

## Core Purpose

Cultivate institutional policy judgment: the ability to design, evaluate, and govern rules, programs, policies, and institutions under uncertainty, disagreement, implementation constraints, and political conflict.

## Governing Principles

1. Public problems must be diagnosed before they are solved.
2. Market failure is not the only justification for policy.
3. Government failure is real.
4. Policy instruments shape incentives.
5. Implementation is policy.
6. Distribution matters.
7. Legitimacy matters.
8. Regulation must adapt to changing systems.

## Core Questions

- What is the public problem?
- What mechanism produces it?
- Is the issue a market failure, government failure, rights violation, distributional problem, coordination failure, or capacity problem?
- What policy instruments are available?
- Who benefits, who pays, and who bears risk?
- What institution has authority and capacity?
- How will compliance be monitored?
- What unintended consequences might emerge?
- How should the policy be evaluated after implementation?

## Lesson 1

### Title
Public Problems Must Be Diagnosed Before They Are Solved

### Central Problem
Policy failure often begins with weak diagnosis. A visible problem may have multiple possible mechanisms, and each mechanism implies different instruments, evidence needs, implementation constraints, and risks.
